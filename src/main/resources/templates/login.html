Reset your password

Your account lives only on this computer, so there's no reset email. Instead, start Reqly once with the reset option and it will print a temporary password:

./gradlew bootRun --args='--reqly.reset-password=you@company.com'

Using the installed app on macOS: /Applications/Reqly.app/Contents/MacOS/Reqly --reqly.reset-password=you@company.com. Sign in with the temporary password, then change it under your account menu.
