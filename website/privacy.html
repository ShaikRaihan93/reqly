1. Who we are

Reqly is developed and published by Shaik Raihan ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, email hello@reqly.app.

2. The Reqly app

Everything Reqly stores is kept only on your device, in the reqly-data folder in your home directory:

  • Account details: your name, email address and a salted hash of your password (BCrypt). Your password itself is never stored. This account exists only on your computer.
  • Your workspace: collections, requests, environments, scripts and test definitions.
  • Request history: recent requests you sent, including response bodies, so you can review and compare them.
  • Secrets: environment variable values, request headers and authentication settings are encrypted at rest with a key generated on, and kept on, your device.
  • Backups and logs: local database backups and log files, which never contain your passwords or tokens.

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. The only network traffic it creates is the API requests you choose to send, which go directly from your computer to the servers you specify.

3. This website

This is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Like any web host, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.

4. Purchases and licenses

When you buy a license, payments are handled by our payment provider, [payment provider], which processes your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy. We receive only what is needed to issue and support your license: your name, email address, country and license details.

5. When you contact us

The contact form on this site doesn't send anything to us directly: it opens your own email app. When you email us, we use your name, email address and message only to reply and to provide support.

6. Third parties

We do not sell or share personal data. APIs you call through Reqly are operated by their own owners and are subject to their terms and privacy policies.

7. Retention and deletion

App data stays on your device until you delete it: clear history in the app, delete collections and environments, or remove the reqly-data folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder automatically. We keep license and support emails for as long as your license is active and as required by tax law, then delete them.

8. Security

Reqly only accepts connections from your own computer, requires you to sign in, rejects requests from other websites, encrypts stored secrets and never writes credentials to its log files. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your computer and account protected.

9. Your rights

Because app data stays on your device, you can access, export, correct or delete it at any time without contacting us. Under laws such as the GDPR you may ask us to access, correct or delete the limited data we hold about you (for licensing and support), or object to its use. Email hello@reqly.app; you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Children

Reqly is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 16.

11. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top.
