Hi, I'm Shaik

I spend my days testing software: checking APIs, automating regression suites and chasing down the bugs nobody else sees. API clients are the tool I open more than any other, and over the years I grew tired of the same trade-offs.

The popular tools kept getting heavier. They wanted me signed in to a cloud account, synced my collections and tokens to servers I didn't control, and buried the basics under menus. I just wanted to send a request, check the response and move on.

So I built Reqly: a fast API client that keeps everything on your own machine, makes writing tests as easy as writing a sentence, and still has the power features — scripts, data-driven runs, request chaining — that real testing needs.

What Reqly stands for

Your data is yours

Collections, secrets and responses stay on your computer. No telemetry, no tracking.

Fast and focused

Common tasks take one click or one line. Everything else is one shortcut away.

Made for testing

Assertions, captures and runners are first-class, not afterthoughts.

Fair and independent

Free for personal use, paid by the professionals who rely on it.

What's next

Windows and Linux apps, a command-line runner for CI pipelines, OpenAPI import and optional self-hosted team workspaces are all on the roadmap. If something would make Reqly better for you, I'd genuinely love to hear it.
