Pricing questions

Can I use the free plan at work?

You can evaluate Reqly at work for as long as you need to decide. For ongoing professional use, please buy a Pro or Team license. It keeps Reqly independent and actively developed.

Is my data stored differently on paid plans?

No. On every plan, your workspace stays on your own computer. Licensing never requires uploading your collections or secrets.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card payments and invoices for teams. Get in touch through the contact page and we'll set you up.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Reqly isn't right for you, ask within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.

Do you offer discounts?

Students, teachers and open-source maintainers can use Pro for free. Tell us about your project.
